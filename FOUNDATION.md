# Ratatoskr Foundation Charter

## Mission
To develop and maintain resilient, decentralized communication
infrastructure for emergency, humanitarian, and civil defense use.

## Principles
- **Neutral infrastructure:** The network serves the people, not the powerful.
- **No central authority:** Resilience through decentralization.
- **Privacy by default:** Encryption is not an option, it's the standard.
- **SOS immunity:** Saving a life takes precedence over social moderation.

## Governance
- No single entity controls the network.
- Protocol changes require public review.
- SOS cryptographic trust is managed transparently.

## Exclusions
The Foundation may publicly disassociate from entities that use the
Ratatoskr name or branding for surveillance or repression.
